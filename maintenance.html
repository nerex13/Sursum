Common Questions

Maintenance without unnecessary complexity.

Do I have to use ongoing maintenance?

No. A completed website can remain live without an ongoing maintenance arrangement. Support is available when the business needs it.

Can I request just one update?

Yes. Small or one-time updates can be handled separately instead of requiring recurring support.

Can new pages be added later?

Yes. A website can grow with new services, portfolio work, information, customer flows, or additional pages.

Is maintenance only for Sursum-built sites?

Existing websites can also be reviewed. Whether Sursum can maintain or modify the site depends on how it is currently built and hosted.
